LAP captures rich track-day telemetry on your phone — lap times, sector splits, and the GPS + motion data your coach actually needs to find time.

WHAT IT DOES
• High-rate GPS capture (up to 25 Hz with a paired RaceBox receiver, or your phone's built-in receiver as a fallback)
• Motion sensors (IMU) recorded alongside GPS so cornering, braking, and gear-shift behaviour is reviewable
• Session bundles uploaded to the LAP backend where lap detection and sector splits are computed server-side
• Coaching prompts surface during a session when the rule engine flags an opportunity (no local fakery — rules are authored server-side)
• Crash-safe recording — a phone reboot, app kill, or airplane-mode toggle mid-session never loses more than the in-memory tail (a few seconds)

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
• No on-device lap detection — laps and sectors are computed server-side from raw GPS so the algorithm stays consistent across drivers and devices
• No coaching logic baked into the app — all rules ship from the backend so updates land without an app release
• No sample fusion before upload — the raw streams are preserved per the data-quality contract so server-side analysis sees what actually happened

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 9 (Pie) or later
• Location permission (foreground + background) so a session keeps recording when the screen is off
• Notification permission so the foreground-service notification can keep the OS from killing the recording
• Optional: Bluetooth permission and a paired RaceBox peripheral for higher-rate, higher-accuracy GPS

PRIVACY
• Sessions stay local until you upload them; nothing is sent in the background
• Telemetry opt-out toggle in Settings disables crash reporting and product analytics SDKs at runtime
• No advertising IDs collected
